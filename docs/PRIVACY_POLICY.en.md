# Privacy Policy (Codex Blanche Android)

**English** | [日本語](PRIVACY_POLICY.md)

**Last updated: May 7, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the “Codex Blanche” Android application (package name `com.tamarilog.codexblanche`, the “App”) handles personal and related data. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

## 1. Operator

The App is primarily distributed as open-source software via [Tamari-log/Codex-Blanche-App](https://github.com/Tamari-log/Codex-Blanche-App). If you obtain builds from another distributor, review their privacy disclosures as well.

## 2. Data handled by the App (overview)

The App processes chat history, settings, API keys (if you enter them), and optional Google account tokens **primarily on your device** (app storage / DataStore, etc.). The App, by itself, **does not automatically upload a user profile** to servers operated by this project (except as needed to reach third-party APIs described below).

## 3. Third-party AI services (Gemini / OpenAI, etc.)

1. When you send messages, **conversation content**, and text or metadata derived from attachments, may be sent to the AI Provider you select.  
2. Those providers process data under **their own privacy policies and terms**. Please read their documents before use.  
3. If you store API keys in the App, they are kept **on your device** for authentication. The Operator typically does not receive your keys.

## 4. Google Drive (optional)

1. If you enable Drive sync, the App may use Google sign-in and the Google Drive API to read/write backup files (for example JSON).  
2. That processing occurs under your control and OAuth scopes and is also subject to Google’s policies (for example the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)).

## 5. Networking and logs

1. The App uses the Internet to call AI APIs, Drive APIs, and related services.  
2. Operating systems and **debug builds** may emit additional logs depending on your build configuration.

## 6. Attachments (images, documents)

Files you select are generally processed on-device and may be transformed before being sent to an AI Provider. If files contain **sensitive personal data or trade secrets**, review each provider’s data-handling practices carefully.

## 7. Children

The App is not directed at children. Parental guidance is recommended. Follow local age requirements where they apply.

## 8. Security

The App follows common mobile-development practices, but **no Internet transmission or on-device storage is perfectly secure**. You are responsible for managing API keys and risks from device loss or compromise.

## 9. Changes

We may update this Policy by revising this file in the repository. Continued use after updates may constitute acceptance of the revised Policy where permitted by law.

## 10. Contact

Questions or concerns may be raised via [Codex-Blanche-App Issues](https://github.com/Tamari-log/Codex-Blanche-App/issues) or other channels described in the repository.
